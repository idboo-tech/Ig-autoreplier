- "Up to 5,000 players" around you ⚠️ the studio's own pitch
- Clans: up to **100 members**, with a base you buy and upgrade ✅
- Paid items are **cosmetics only** ✅
- Each server **wipes every 7 days**: cash, weapons and base reset, cosmetics stay ⚠️ from fan wikis quoting the dev FAQ, check in game
- Steam reviews are **Mixed** (~48% positive) ⚠️ expect a few "the game is broken" comments
- From your own session: robbing shops, gunfights with police and rival thieves, a thieves-only mode, face + clothes editor, cars, weapons, clan base
