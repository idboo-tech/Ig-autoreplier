## The rules to copy
1. **Open a curiosity gap in the first line** ("deleted", "most people think", "don't know", "imagine").
2. **Label every claim** on screen: ✅ مؤكد · ⚠️ تقارير · 📜 تاريخ · 🔮 توقع ديالي. Trust is why #5 got 1.9M.
3. **Plant an open loop** early and pay it off at the end; add one **re-hook** in the middle.
4. **Numbers beat adjectives**: "$1.25 for each item", "150 GB left".
5. **GTA 5 vs GTA 6** framing works for carousels; one change per slide, with the source line.
6. **Saves come from guides** (#4); **shares from "you didn't know this about what you bought"** (#6).
7. **End with a question people can answer in one word** ("which edition?", "why did they delete it?").
8. Long videos become **series** ("Part 1/2/3"); each part ends on the next part's hook.
