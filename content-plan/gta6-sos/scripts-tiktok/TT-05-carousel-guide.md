## Facts
- **Hit markers** (preview reports): white = a hit · yellow = enemy down / incapacitated · red = kill ✅ previews
- **Focus mode** slows the action and marks lethal spots **red** and non-lethal spots **yellow**; lethal vs non-lethal can change your **Criminal Profile** ✅ previews
- Enemies show on the minimap **only in Focus** ✅ previews
- **6 wanted stars**; icons under them = what police know: face, clothes, weapon, vehicle, partner, CCTV ✅
- ❌ Skip "cheat code" slides: no GTA 6 codes exist yet (TT-06 debunks them).
