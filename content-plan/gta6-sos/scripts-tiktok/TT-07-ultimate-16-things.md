## Facts
- Standard **$79.99**, Ultimate **$99.99** ✅
- Ultimate = **16 single-player extras** ✅: 5 vehicles ('95 **Grotti Cheetah**, '67 **Vapid Dominator Buggy**, Dinka Enduro motorbike, Shitzu Squalo boat, Crest kayak), 2 weapon sets (Hawk & Little Morgan **revolvers**, personalised pistols), 2 clothing collections, **5 shops** (Rideout Customs, Sara's Unisex Salon, Stock 305, Electric Fang Tattoo, One-Eyed Willie's), **2 activities** (the PTT Youngin$ raid, a classic-car collection side job).
- $20 ÷ 16 = **$1.25 per extra** (maths).
- On the official list: **no extra story mission and no extra region** ✅ (side activities only).
- ⚠️ Reported from Rockstar's store text: items **unlock chapter by chapter**, and the Cheetah is pitched for later in the story.
