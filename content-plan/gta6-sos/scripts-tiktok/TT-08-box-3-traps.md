## Facts
- The box = a **download code**, no disc ✅ (Rockstar Support). Boxes can pre-load from **12 Nov** ✅
- Trap 1: the box is **Standard only**; there is **no boxed Ultimate** ✅
- Trap 2: the free **GTA+ month** is a **digital** PS pre-order bonus, so the box doesn't include it ⚠️ reported
- Trap 3: **PS5 codes are region-locked** to your account's country ✅ widely reported; Xbox codes aren't
- Bonus: once redeemed, the game is **tied to your account**: nothing to resell or trade later ✅ (how codes work)
- Morocco: most players use a **French** PSN account → buy a French code or French PSN credit.
