**Inspired by:** #5 @blingblingzzxx, part 3 of 3 (the console chart) · **Type:** Explainer + chart · **Length:** ~35 s

## Facts
- Download **up to 212 GB** (Rockstar via Giga.de); a PS Store leak said 210 GB ⚠️
- Usable space: base PS5 ≈ **667 GB** ✅ · Xbox Series S ≈ **364 GB** ✅ · Series X ≈ **802 GB** ✅ · PS5 Slim (1 TB) and PS5 Pro (2 TB) have more ⚠️ round numbers only
- Series S: 212 of 364 GB → **more than half**, about **150 GB left** (maths)
- A **launch patch** is likely and its size is unknown 🔮
- Pre-load: digital **10 Nov**, boxes **12 Nov** ✅
