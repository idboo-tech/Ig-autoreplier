# TT-01 · They deleted GTA 6's age rating. Why?

**Inspired by:** #1 @xmassty (rumour reaction, 28 s, 351K) · **Type:** Talking head, curiosity gap · **Length:** ~25 s

## Facts
- A **PEGI 18** listing for GTA 6 appeared on PEGI's website, then was **removed**. Outlets captured its description first: it reportedly mentioned weapons capable of **detailed decapitation and dismemberment**, drug use and sexual content. ⚠️ Reported, not confirmed; PEGI gave no reason.
- The **ESRB** rating in the US is **M (17+)** ✅.
- Keep it clean for Instagram: say the words, show no gore.
- Sources: GTABoom, Digital Trends, IBTimes (see the Notion page / research notes).
