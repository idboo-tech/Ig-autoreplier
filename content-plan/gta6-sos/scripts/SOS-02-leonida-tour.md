## Facts
- Leonida = Rockstar's Florida. The 6 named regions: **Vice City** (Miami), **Leonida Keys** (Florida Keys), **Grassrivers** (Everglades), **Ambrosia** (industry and farming, a sugar refinery), **Port Gellhorn**, **Mount Kalaga** (national park in the north).
- ⚠️ Don't give real-life matches for Port Gellhorn or Mount Kalaga; they aren't confirmed.
- Sources: `research.md` → Story, world and gameplay.
