# GTA 6 SOS #09 · Jason & Lucia: who are they?

**Post:** Wed 11 Nov · 13:00 · **Type:** Story / characters · **Length:** ~30 s · **Chip:** «باقي 8 أيام»

## Facts
- **Lucia Caminos**: the first trailer opens with her in **Leonida Penitentiary**. She's the first female lead in the 3D-era GTA (since GTA III).
- ⚠️ "Her father taught her to fight": that comes from Rockstar's character page. Check it on rockstargames.com before filming, or cut the line.
- **Jason Duval** (Rockstar's page): grew up around grifters and crooks, joined the **Army**, then ended up in the **Keys** working for local drug runners. The launch trailer (15 Oct) leans on his army past.
- Rockstar's pitch: a criminal conspiracy that stretches across Leonida.
- Sources: `research.md` → Story, world and gameplay.
