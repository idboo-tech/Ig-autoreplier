# GTA 6 SOS #05 · Buying in Morocco: no disc + region lock

**Post:** Mon 9 Nov · 13:00 · **Type:** Local warning (high-save, high-send) · **Length:** ~30 s · **Chip:** «باقي 10 أيام»

## Facts
- Rockstar Support: the boxed edition has **no disc**, only a download code. Only the Standard Edition is boxed; boxes from 12 Nov.
- **PS5 codes are region-locked** to the PSN account's country (Sony doesn't let you change an account's region). Xbox codes aren't (widely reported).
- Morocco has no official PS Store, so most players use a **French** account → buy a code or PSN credit from the **same region as your account**.
- Shop listings in Morocco: about 899–999 DH (not official).
- Sources: `research.md` → Buying.
