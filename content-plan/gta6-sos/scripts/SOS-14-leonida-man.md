## Facts
- "Florida Man": a US meme built on weird real news headlines ("Florida Man does…").
- Leonida is Rockstar's Florida; fans read "Leonida Man" as a nod to the meme.
- The first trailer (Dec 2023) is full of scenes that look like real viral Florida videos. ⚠️ Say «الفانز كيقولو»: which exact clip inspired which scene isn't official.
