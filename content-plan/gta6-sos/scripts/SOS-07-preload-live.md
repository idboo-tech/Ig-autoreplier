## Facts
- Rockstar Support: digital pre-load starts 10 Nov, **00:00 local on PS5**, **00:00 GMT on Xbox** (01:00 Morocco). Boxed codes from 12 Nov.
- Nobody can play before **00:00 on 19 Nov** (local time).

- Sources: `research.md` → Launch.
