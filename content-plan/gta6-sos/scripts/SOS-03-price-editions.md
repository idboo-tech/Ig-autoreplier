## Facts
- Standard **$79.99** · Ultimate **$99.99** (UK £69.99, Germany €79.99). ⚠️ Check the price on your own PSN region (France) before filming.
- Pre-order bonus for **both** editions: **Vintage Vice City Pack** ('55 Vapid Stanier, a garage, Vice City outfits and hairstyles, a tropical weapon pattern) for digital purchases **by 20 Nov**.
- Ultimate adds exclusive vehicles, weapons, outfits, businesses and activities; the Ultimate upgrade can be bought later.
