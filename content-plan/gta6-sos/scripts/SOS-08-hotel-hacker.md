## Facts
- **Sept 2022**: about **90 clips** of early GTA 6 footage leaked.
- The hacker, **Arion Kurtaj** (a Lapsus$ member, a teenager at the time), did it while on bail, from a **hotel room**, with an **Amazon Fire Stick**, the **hotel TV** and a **phone** (his laptop had been seized), according to court reporting.
- Rockstar put the cost at about **$5 million**.
- **Dec 2023**: the court gave him an **indefinite hospital order** (secure hospital, no fixed end date).
- Sources: `research.md` → Numbers and history. Don't show the leaked footage itself.
