## Reel formula (use for every reel)

1. **0–2 s, hook:** on-screen text + say it out loud. Start mid-action, no intro.
2. **2–20 s, value:** 3 quick points max, a cut every 2–3 seconds.
3. **Last 3 s, CTA plus signature outro:** "o kan m3akom idboo, ila makontouch 3arfin, daba 3arfin."
4. Always add **subtitles** (many people watch on mute) and a **consistent cover** so the grid looks clean.
5. Aim for **15–35 seconds**. Shorter gets more rewatches.

## Other suggestions

1. **Use comment-to-DM automation** (this repo, `Ig-autoreplier`). "Comment GAME / CODE / SETUP and I'll DM you" brings more comments, which means more reach, and every request gets answered automatically. Use one keyword per post and say it in the reel *and* the caption.
2. **Make Free Game Thursday a real series.** Same thumbnail template, same opening line and same post time each week, plus its own highlight. People should *expect* it.
3. **Try Trial Reels** for riskier ideas (njara, PC vs PS5). Instagram shows them to non-followers first, so a flop doesn't hurt your main feed.
4. **Use Collab posts** with Mouad & Zack and with brands. One post reaches every account's followers.
5. **Repurpose everything.** Post the same reels to TikTok and YouTube Shorts, exported without the IG watermark.
6. **Write SEO captions.** Instagram search reads captions, so add searchable words: *"free game Epic Games Maroc"*, *"meilleur clavier Maroc"*, *"review Oraimo Morocco"*. 3–5 relevant hashtags is enough.
7. **Spend 15 minutes on engagement before and after posting.** Reply to every comment in the first hour, and reply to some comments with a video reply.
8. **Use your comeback for brand deals.** After 2 consistent weeks, screenshot your 14-day reach and views and send them with your outreach emails (drawing tablet brands, Oraimo, Keychron, local shops like star10.ma).
9. **Pin 3 posts** to the top of your grid: the "hello world" intro, your best Free Game post and your best review.
10. **Plan week 3 now.** It's the biggest week of the month: **GTA 6 launch (Nov 19)** and **Black Friday / Steam sales (late Nov)**. Script "Best Black Friday gaming deals in Morocco" and "GTA 6 first impressions" during week 2.
