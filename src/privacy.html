Privacy Policy

idboo autoreplier is a tool run by Idriss (Instagram @idboo_) that answers people who comment certain keywords on @idboo_'s Instagram posts. Last updated: October 27, 2026.

What the app does

When you comment a keyword (for example "FREE" or "GAME") on a post by @idboo_, the app sends you one private message with the link you asked for and posts a short public reply under your comment. If you send @idboo_ a direct message with a keyword, it may reply with the matching link.

What data is used

  • The text of the comment or message, to check whether it contains a keyword.
  • Your Instagram username, to greet you in the reply.
  • The ID of the comment or message, so the same comment is never answered twice.

This data comes from Instagram through Meta's official Instagram API. The app does not read anything else from your account.

What is stored and for how long

Only the IDs of comments and messages that were already answered are stored, on the app's server, to prevent duplicate replies. Comment text and usernames are not stored, apart from short-lived server logs that are deleted automatically within 7 days. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Deleting your data

To have any data about you removed, email contact.idboo@gmail.com or send a DM to @idboo_ with the word "DELETE". It will be removed within 30 days. You can also simply delete your comment on Instagram.
